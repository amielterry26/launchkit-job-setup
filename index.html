The Story

Why I built this

Most people don't struggle with applying — they struggle with getting set up to apply well. The resume isn't ready, the profile is outdated, the job boards aren't configured. This service exists to solve that specific problem, cleanly and efficiently.

I built LaunchKit because the setup work is something I genuinely enjoy, and it's something most job seekers skip or rush. Done properly, it makes everything downstream easier.
